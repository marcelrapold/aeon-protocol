# ADR NNNN — the decision in one line

One sentence saying what this record settles, in the same voice as the title.

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Governs:** the files, directories or behaviour this decision constrains

## Context

The forces that made a decision necessary, written before the answer is known: what the project
needed, what was already true, what pushed in opposite directions. A reader who disagrees with the
decision should still recognise the problem.

## Decision

What the project now does, stated plainly and in the present tense. Name the alternative you
rejected and the property that decided against it — an ADR without a rejected alternative is a
description, not a decision.

## Consequences

- What the project gains.
- What the project now has to live with, including the costs you dislike.
- The obligations the decision creates for future contributors, and where those obligations are
  written down so they are not forgotten.
